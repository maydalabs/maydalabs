# MaydaLabs Commercial Operating Model

_Status: supervised operating baseline, 1 September 2026_

MaydaLabs is Mehmet's commercial product studio. It is the income-generation
lane around the work, not a wrapper that turns every repository into a client
case or every draft into marketing.

The canonical live queue is private in Abidin at
`config/mayda-operating-model.json`. This document states the public-repository
contract so the website, cases, launch work, and future commercial decisions do
not drift away from it.

## Operating order

1. **Sell** — choose one bounded offer and buyer, verify a real need, then earn
   a conversation, proposal, and revenue outcome.
2. **Prove** — package shipped products and client work with sources,
   permission, results, and limitations intact.
3. **Distribute** — publish useful proof and founder/company thinking through
   channels that can be maintained and measured.
4. **Capital** — research grants, accelerators, or investors only after one
   owned product has an explicit venture-scale thesis.

This order matters. Three researched companies are still three research leads,
not three qualified opportunities. Six channel variants are still drafts, not
distribution. Several strong products are still not a fundraising thesis.

## Current offer hypotheses

These are hypotheses until Mehmet selects one for a supervised commercial
cycle. The website must not imply that all three are standardized services.

### Product rescue and launch sprint

For a founder or small product team with a real product near launch or stuck
between prototype and credible release. The outcome is a bounded release plan
and the smallest credible production path across product, engineering,
measurement, and launch evidence.

Current proof: HodlStay and Satoshi Gazette.

### Marketplace foundation or rebuild

For a marketplace founder whose buyer journey, operations, evidence, or launch
loop is fragmented. The outcome is a working foundation with explicit
boundaries around identity, inventory, transactions, operations, and
measurement.

Current proof: HodlStay and Sofra, with Sofra clearly labeled as private Phase
1 rather than a live transaction business.

### Editorial and information operating system

For a specialist publication, research team, or expert-led company that needs
evidence to move into reliable public output. The outcome is a human-controlled
intake, evidence, editorial, publication, distribution, and learning system.

Current proof: Satoshi Gazette and the private Abidin control plane.

## Portfolio truth

- **HodlStay** is the strongest external client proof. Existing public evidence
  may be used; a testimonial remains permissioned and unsent.
- **Satoshi Gazette** is an owned independent publication and flagship product.
  MaydaLabs may describe what it built while preserving a visible separation
  between editorial decisions and commercial client work.
- **Sofra** is strong marketplace architecture proof, but private Phase 1 with
  no claim of real payments or a public operating marketplace.
- **Mortal Vault** is a private, unaudited alpha. It must retain the
  meaningful-funds warning and cannot be described as production ready.
- **The Bitcoin Way concept** is an unofficial, private/noindex job-application
  artifact. It is never client work, an endorsement, or MaydaLabs portfolio
  evidence.
- **Automotive concept starter** stays on hold until its identity, scope, and
  implementation are reconciled.
- **AirBTC online bridge** is retired history, not flagship proof.
- **Abidin** is private operating infrastructure. Its capabilities may be
  described without exposing private state, records, or client information.

## Channel order

Founder-led LinkedIn and the owned site should carry the first commercial
cycle. The MaydaLabs company page and X can follow after ownership/profile
readiness is verified. Instagram and Facebook remain a hold until a repeatable
visual format or a specific paid audience justifies the maintenance burden.

Paid ads come after all of the following are true:

- one offer and one buyer profile are chosen;
- one landing path states that offer clearly;
- conversion tracking is verified;
- the same message has produced useful organic or outreach evidence;
- a narrow budget, stop rule, and exact conversion are approved.

## Capital boundary

MaydaLabs as a broad service studio is not automatically a YC or venture case.
Before accelerator, Crunchbase, investor, or grant research becomes active,
one owned product must have:

- a chosen buyer and painful problem;
- a wedge and product thesis;
- honest traction or a clearly labeled lack of traction;
- an evidence-backed market claim;
- a defined capital need;
- a reason external capital improves the outcome rather than merely financing
  unfocused work.

Until then, capital research is a deliberate hold. Client revenue and stronger
proof are the nearer source of leverage.

## Consequential-action boundary

Nothing in this model authorizes a post, message, email, testimonial request,
ad, account creation, proposal, investment application, deployment, or
schedule. Abidin may research, draft, compare, and prepare exact material.
Mehmet approves the target and payload before any external action, and the
result is recorded only after it actually happens.
