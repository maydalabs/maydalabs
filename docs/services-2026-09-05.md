# Plain services — September 5, 2026

Local implementation of Mehmet's approved services reset. Not a deployment
receipt. Production remains source 4c2b2ea until a separately approved release.

## Approved direction

- Five services: Websites & online stores; Custom software; AI & automation;
  Email & customer journeys; Fixes & ongoing support.
- Broad, plain description of websites, software and connected business tools.
  This supersedes the two-offer AI/Bitcoin sales pitch and broadens the previous
  AI-only headline; it does not erase those historical decisions.
- Services navigation, Tell us what you need primary CTA, See our work secondary.
  No account, diagnostic or pilot is required to enquire. Scope, price and
  responsibilities are agreed per project. No fixed prices or time guarantees.
- Explicit user exception: retain the Bitcoin dashboard near the homepage
  bottom, after services, selected work and process. Keep its real data source.
  Remove the separate Bitcoin payments service, not genuine case history or
  existing payment/billing functionality.
- Keep the current visual system, typography, colors and logo. MaydaOS stays
  private beta with existing page/action/database gates and separate portal.

## Implementation

Shared EN/TR/FR service data in lib/services.ts drives the homepage and /services.
The index links to five service anchors, each with examples and possible
deliverables, explicitly examples rather than client results. /programs redirects
to /services, retaining locale. /approach renders the same new Services content
with /services canonical metadata: this avoids a reverse-redirect loop for
browsers that cached the old permanent /services → /approach redirect.
Sitemap lists /services, not the compatibility alias.

Homepage now leads with plain work, five services, HodlStay and SG, and a simple
three-step process. Removed the repeated SG hero/proof block, sales illustrations,
stack/rails strip and unused founder-draft quote from this route. Existing assets
and off-route components remain recoverable; no client product was changed.

About, contact, company structured data, navigation, footer copy, proof-page
framing and case CTAs follow the broader direction. SG remains owned and
editorially independent; HodlStay remains Client build · Live. Private Sofra and
Mortal Vault case limitations are unchanged. The founder hiring profile remains
available; historical Bitcoin experience is not rewritten.

The optional Multiplier Map keeps its saved result keys and rubric unchanged.
Display labels now describe a scoped project or improvement, not the retired
named packages. Historical maps are not rewritten. Intake actions, database
schema, credentials, auth, pilots, invoices and payment code are untouched.
