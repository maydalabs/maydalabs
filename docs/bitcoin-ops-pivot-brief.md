_Status: local working record, 2 September 2026. Decisions from Mehmet's

## Decisions (made by Mehmet, 2 Sep 2026)

1. **SG is a product of the company, not the company.** MaydaLabs is a
   Bitcoin-first operations company; Satoshi Gazette is its flagship
   product and living demo.
2. **Bitcoin-first, not Bitcoin-only.** The company leads with and
   prioritizes Bitcoin companies; it does not refuse other clients. SG's
   own charter stays Bitcoin-only.
3. **Money clock: under six months.** Services revenue must start now;
   product extraction follows paid engagements.
4. Consulting is the reconnaissance for infrastructure: the repeated pain
   across two to three paid engagements chooses what gets productized.
   Candidates to watch (not build yet): BTCPay operations layer,
   treasury-operations tooling, the agent-ops system itself.
5. Ruled out for now: anything holding other people's funds (custody,
   wallets, inheritance — Mortal Vault stays a lab artifact), Lightning
   protocol work, mining hardware/hosting, licensed activity.
6. **Cadence:** production sprint days 1–8; first five outreach notes sent
   by Mehmet around day 10; batches of five per week after; copy and
   design iterate from real replies. No further full site rebuilds.

## The two offers

1. **Evidence-gated AI operations** (lead offer). Install a system that
   runs a company's content, research, or internal workflow: AI produces,
   every claim is source-linked or marked unverified, and nothing external
   happens without a human approval. Proof: Satoshi Gazette runs on this
   system, publicly inspectable.
2. **Bitcoin payments engineering** (second offer). BTCPay Server
   deployment, integration, invoice/webhook lifecycle, settlement and
   payout reconciliation, hardening. Proof: HodlStay's production payment
   lifecycle, built end to end.
