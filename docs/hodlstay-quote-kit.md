# HodlStay client quote — outreach kit

Goal: one short, attributable quote from the HodlStay client for the
case study page. **Nothing ships until they approve the exact wording
and attribution in writing.** The drafts below exist to make the ask
effortless — the client can pick one, edit freely, or write their own.

## The ask (send as-is or trim)

**EN**

> Hi — the HodlStay case study on maydalabs.com is getting real reader
> traffic, and one thing would make it materially stronger: a sentence
> or two from you. Nothing formal. If it's easier, pick or edit one of
> the three drafts below — you have full veto on wording, and we'll
> show your name/role exactly as you want it (or keep it
> company-level). Takes two minutes; happy to jump on a call instead.

**TR**

> Selam — maydalabs.com'daki HodlStay vaka çalışması gerçek okur
> trafiği alıyor ve onu belirgin şekilde güçlendirecek tek şey sizden
> bir-iki cümle. Resmî bir şey değil. Kolaylık olsun diye aşağıya üç
> taslak bıraktım — dilediğinizi seçip düzenleyebilirsiniz, son söz
> tamamen sizde. İsim/unvan nasıl görünsün siz söyleyin (şirket
> düzeyinde de kalabilir). İki dakikalık iş; istersen arayıp da
> halledebiliriz.

## Candidate quotes (drafts for the client to edit or replace)

1. > "MaydaLabs took HodlStay from an idea to a live marketplace —
   > bookings, payments, three languages — and handed it over so we
   > could actually run it."

2. > "What stood out was the discipline: short cycles, working software
   > every week, and documentation that meant the handover was real,
   > not a goodbye."

3. > "They didn't just build the product; they built the measurement
   > and lifecycle systems around it, so we could see what was working
   > from day one."

## What we need back

- Final wording (theirs, always)
- Attribution as they want it displayed: name, role, company — or
  company-only ("The HodlStay team")
- Written OK (email reply is enough) — file it before publishing

## Where it goes once approved

The HodlStay case page hero rail (`app/[lang]/case-studies/hodlstay/page.tsx`)
gets a quote block under the engagement rail, and the same quote can
anchor the work index card. Localize with the client (TR/FR renderings
also need their sign-off, or we show the original with a translation
note).
