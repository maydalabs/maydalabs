## Founder post — EN (LinkedIn, personal)

For the last seven months I've been running an experiment on myself.

I built a Bitcoin publication — Satoshi Gazette — and then built the
system that operates it. AI drafts every piece from collected sources.
Every claim is linked to a source or it's marked unverified and doesn't
run as fact. And nothing — not a story, not a post — goes out without me
clicking approve.

34 pieces later, my honest conclusion: the bottleneck in most small
companies isn't AI capability. It's trust. Nobody wants a bot that can
embarrass them in public or invent a number in a client report.

So that's what MaydaLabs sells now: I install this same system for
Bitcoin companies — content desks, research pipelines, payment
operations. The machine does the volume. A human at your company owns
every consequence.

The whole thing is inspectable: maydalabs.com/proof

If your team's week disappears into production work, my pilot is small
on purpose: one workflow, fixed price, running in your accounts in a
month.
