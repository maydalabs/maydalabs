## What we do

MaydaLabs installs AI-run operations for Bitcoin companies. AI does the
production; every claim is linked to its source; nothing external happens
without a human approval. Our own publication, Satoshi Gazette, runs on
this system in public: 34 pieces published, every one human-approved,
every distribution action recorded against a public URL.

## Offer 1 — Evidence-gated AI operations

**For:** Bitcoin companies where content, research, or internal ops eat
the team's week — newsletters, market notes, listings, support docs,
reporting.

**Pilot (3–4 weeks, from $2,500 fixed):**
- Week 1: scope one workflow precisely — what comes in, what goes out,
  who approves. Install the pipeline in your accounts.
- Weeks 2–3: operate it daily. AI produces; your review takes minutes;
  sourcing rules are enforced by the pipeline, not by promises.
- Week 4: plain report — output volume, approval latency, source
  coverage, cost per piece.

**Then:** from $1,000/month to operate and extend — or keep the system
and walk. It's in your accounts either way.

## Offer 2 — Bitcoin payments engineering

**For:** companies and serious merchants that want to accept Bitcoin

**Scope:** BTCPay deployment or rescue, product integration, the full
invoice-to-payout lifecycle, signed webhook verification, settlement and

**Proof:** HodlStay's production payment system — built end to end.

**Pricing:** fixed price after a 30-minute technical call.
